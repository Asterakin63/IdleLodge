Profane à la porte du Temple, votre personnage devient Apprenti, puis Compagnon, puis Maître. Pierre après pierre, bâtissez votre Temple intérieur… même quand le téléphone dort.

## English

**Name**: Idle Lodge

**Short description**:
Work your rough ashlar: an idle game along the paths of Freemasonry.

**Full description**:
A seeker at the door of the Temple, your character rises from Apprentice to Fellowcraft to Master. Stone by stone, build your inner Temple — even while your phone sleeps.

• Over twenty skills: Quarrying, Timberwork, Hiram's Founding, Architecture, Symbolism, Alchemy, Provisioning and Banqueting, Charity…
• Trials against the vices and the Darkness, up to the three Ruffians.
• The initiatory journeys of Ritual: trials by Earth, Air, Water and Fire, the Winding Staircase, the Middle Chamber.
• Lodges to join, daily quests, Jacob's Ladder, the Isle of the East and the Temple of Solomon to complete.
• Seasonal feasts: Summer and Winter St John's, the equinoxes.
• Furnish your Private Lodge and collect emblems: pelican, phoenix, bee…

Idle play: start your work, close the app, come back to reap the fruits of your labour.
No ads, no in-app purchases, no Internet connection, no data collection.

Free software (GPL-3.0), derived from Idle Fantasy by Tristin Baker. A work of fiction inspired by Masonic symbolism; not affiliated with any Grand Lodge or obedience.
